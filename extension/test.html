🎓 LectureScribe Test Bed

This page embeds a sample public Vimeo video to test the LectureScribe browser extension.

What to check on this page:

  • Floating Action Badge: You should see a 🎓 LectureScribe #76979871 pill badge floating over the top-right corner of the player above. Click it to inspect the quick action dropdown!
  • Extension Toolbar Icon: The extension icon in your browser toolbar should show a badge badge number 1.
  • Popup Inspector: Click the toolbar icon to view the detected video title, ID, cache status, and workspace launch buttons.
  • External Link Test: Right-click this link: Sample Vimeo Link and check the context menu for 🎓 Open Vimeo Video in LectureScribe.
